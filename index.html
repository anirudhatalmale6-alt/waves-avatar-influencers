Questions

Everything you need to know.

How long does delivery take?

Most videos are delivered within 5-12 hours. Rush delivery is available for an extra fee.

Can I choose any avatar?

Yes! We have over 1,200 AI avatars available. Browse our gallery above or describe the type of presenter you'd like and we'll find the perfect match.

What if I don't like the result?

Every order includes one free revision. You'll receive a watermarked preview first. If you need changes, we'll adjust the script, emotion, or background at no extra cost. Additional revisions are just £3 each.

How long can the video be?

Quick Videos are up to 10 seconds. Standard Videos are up to 60 seconds. Premium Videos can be up to 3 minutes. Need something longer? Contact us for a custom quote.

Can I use the same avatar for multiple videos?

Absolutely. Many of our clients use the same avatar across their entire campaign. This builds brand recognition just like using a real influencer, but at a fraction of the cost.

Do you write the script for me?

Yes! If you're not sure what to say, just describe your product or service and we'll write a professional script for you. Included in the price.

Can I add my logo or branding?

Yes. Mention it in the notes section and we'll add your logo, brand colours, or any overlay text to the video.

What languages are available?

Our avatars can speak in 40+ languages with natural lip-sync. Just specify the language in your brief.

Can I use my own face or model?

Yes! Upload a clear, front-facing selfie or photo and we'll create an AI avatar that looks like you or your chosen model. Perfect for personal branding and consistent content campaigns.
